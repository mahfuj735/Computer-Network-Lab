## 🎓 Report Structure & Methodology
Each experiment in the report follows the standard IEEE/ABET laboratory engineering format:
1. **Experiment Title & Objective**: Specific technical competencies to acquire.
2. **Requirements**: Exact hardware models (routers, switches, cables) and software versions.
3. **Network Design & Schematics**: Architectural layout in Cisco Packet Tracer.
4. **Step-by-Step Procedure**: Systematic instructions for reproducing the experiment.
5. **Results & Screenshots**: Simulation outputs, packet tracing, and ICMP ping verification.
6. **Discussion & Conclusion**: Engineering trade-offs and performance analysis.
